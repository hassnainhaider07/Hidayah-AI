# Hidayah AI

Hidayah AI is an AI-powered Islamic companion designed to help Muslims strengthen their faith and improve their daily Islamic lifestyle.

## Features

- Daily Quran Ayah with Translation
- Personalized Islamic Schedule
- Prayer Tracking
- Daily Good Deed Challenges
- Islamic Habit Tracker
- Smart Reminders & Notifications
- AI-Powered Islamic Assistant

## Vision

To help Muslims worldwide build consistent Islamic habits and stay connected with the Quran and Sunnah through modern technology.

## Future Roadmap

- AI Schedule Optimization
- Family Accounts
- Quran Memorization Tracker
- Islamic Learning Plans
- Community Features
- Mobile Apps (Android & iOS)
